Two things were deliberate. Scout never invents a stop — the only extras it offers are real, mapped places that genuinely sit on your route, tagged as optional and left out of the day’s time and cost. And when there’s a hole in the day, it says so rather than quietly filling it with something nobody asked for.

It’s a proposal, never a decision. Nothing touches your trip until someone says so.

Let Scout plan your days

Open a trip → Things to do → Scout: plan our days on a laptop, or Make a plan on your phone. Works best once a few people have voted.

 

A few hundred people are seeing this before anyone else. If it gets a day wrong — a bad drive time, stops in a silly order, a reason that doesn’t hold up — tell me. That’s the whole point of sending it to you first.
